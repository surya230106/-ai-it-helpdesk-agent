NETWORK TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Symptoms: connected to Wi-Fi but no internet, DNS errors, intermittent access, VPN connectivity problems.

Safe checks:
1. Confirm whether another device can access the same network.
2. Reconnect to Wi-Fi or reseat Ethernet.
3. Restart the affected device if safe.
4. Test a known website to distinguish local app problems from network problems.
5. Check VPN/proxy settings when organization access is affected.
6. If many users are affected, escalate as a possible shared network outage.

Do not ask users to provide passwords, MFA codes, private keys, or other secrets.
