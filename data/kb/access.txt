LOGIN AND ACCESS TROUBLESHOOTING

For login or access failures:
1. Confirm the username is correct without asking for the password.
2. Look for account-lock or access-denied messages.
3. Confirm the device has network/VPN access.
4. Retry after restarting the browser or clearing a stale session when appropriate.
5. Escalate permission changes, identity verification, or locked-account cases to an administrator.

Never request passwords or MFA codes.
